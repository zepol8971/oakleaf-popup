Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions before booking? Here are answers to the most common inquiries.

What areas do you serve?

We primarily serve Clay, Duval, and St. Johns counties including Oakleaf Plantation, Orange Park, Middleburg, Fleming Island, Jacksonville, and Palatka.

Are your playlists family-friendly?

Yes, 100%! All pool parties, CDD events, and community festivals feature clean radio edits suitable for all ages while keeping the energy soaring.

What kind of interactive games do you run?

We tailor games to the crowd: music trivia, crowd dance challenges, limbo, potato sack races, tug-of-war, water games, and raffle announcements!

What are the power requirements?

We just need a standard 120V outdoor or indoor outlet within 75-100 feet of the setup area. We supply all commercial-grade cords and surge protectors.
